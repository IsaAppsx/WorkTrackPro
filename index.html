Support & FAQ

Got questions? We're here to help.

Q1: Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. WorkTrack Pro is 100% offline-first. You don't need to sign up, create an account, or have an active internet connection to log your shifts.

Q2: How is my work data kept secure?

Your privacy is our top priority. All your timesheets, salary details, and profiles are stored securely and locally on your device. We never collect or access your data.

Q3: Can I track multiple jobs or freelance clients?

Absolutely! You can create multiple separate job profiles, each with its own specific hourly wage, currency, and work log history.

Q4: Can I export my timesheets for my employer?

Yes, you can easily generate and export detailed work hour and income reports in both PDF and CSV (Excel) formats.
